\# Internet of Things



\## Overview



This section contains practical Internet of Things (IoT) coursework completed as part of my Level 5 HND in Digital Technologies (Cybersecurity).



The project focused on the design and implementation of an IoT Smart Waste Bin solution intended to improve waste collection through sensor-based monitoring and data-driven decision-making.



The work combined IoT architecture design, Arduino hardware, sensors, communications, cloud/platform concepts, testing, security considerations, scalability planning, and physical prototyping.



\## Practical Project



\### IoT Smart Waste Bin



The project addressed a scenario in which waste bins were not always emptied at the appropriate time, resulting in overflowing bins and inefficient fixed collection routes.



The proposed IoT solution used real-time fill-level monitoring to provide earlier warnings and enable waste collection to be based on actual bin status.



The project considered deployment from an initial prototype through to a larger environment of approximately 50 smart bins.

\## IoT Architecture



A five-layer IoT architecture was selected for the proposed solution:



1\. \*\*Perception Layer\*\* – sensors and physical smart-bin hardware

2\. \*\*Network Layer\*\* – communication between devices and the platform

3\. \*\*Processing Layer\*\* – telemetry processing, rules, storage, and middleware

4\. \*\*Application Layer\*\* – dashboards, alerts, reporting, and incident management

5\. \*\*Business Layer\*\* – operational KPIs, collection policies, and service optimisation



The layered design was intended to support maintainability, security, reliability, and future expansion.
