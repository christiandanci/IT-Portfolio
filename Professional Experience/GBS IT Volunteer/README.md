\# IT Support Volunteer



\## Organisation



GBS Leeds



\## Role



IT Support Volunteer



\---



\# Overview



Volunteered within the IT department, assisting students, lecturers and staff with day-to-day IT support while gaining practical experience in a live educational IT environment.



The placement provided exposure to first-line IT support, device deployment, classroom technology, troubleshooting and enterprise IT support processes.



\---



\# Responsibilities



\## First-Line IT Support



\- Assisted students and staff with basic IT issues at the IT support desk.

\- Provided friendly front-line customer support.

\- Directed users to the appropriate knowledge base using the department's QR code system where appropriate.

\- Escalated issues outside my responsibilities to members of the IT team.



\---



\## Device Deployment



\- Issued laptops to students.

\- Processed returned laptops.

\- Assisted with preparing laptops for future use.



\---



\## Campus Technical Support



\- Installed USB drives containing the required screensaver configuration on campus computers.

\- Verified successful installation after deployment.

\- Assisted with projector troubleshooting across classrooms.

\- Assisted with classroom firewall update activities following the IT team's procedures.



\---



\## Technical Exposure



During my volunteer placement I also gained valuable exposure to enterprise IT support operations, including:



\- Observing Jira Service Management being used to manage support tickets.

\- Understanding first-line IT support workflows.

\- Following documented troubleshooting procedures.

\- Using the department's knowledge base to assist users.

\- Working alongside experienced IT technicians in a live support environment.
