### Windows Command Prompt (CMD)

Used Windows Command Prompt during field support to assist with troubleshooting and device identification.

- `ipconfig /all` – Verify IP configuration, DNS settings, MAC address and network adapter information.
- `wmic bios get serialnumber` – Retrieve the device serial number for asset verification and documentation.
- `ping` – Test network connectivity to devices and services.
- `hostname` – Confirm the computer name.
- `systeminfo` – Gather Windows system information when required.

## Troubleshooting Approach

When completing field assignments, I followed a structured process:

1. Confirm the reported task or fault.
2. Inspect the equipment and connections.
3. Follow the approved technical instructions.
4. Complete the replacement, configuration or troubleshooting work.
5. Test the equipment and associated software.
6. Document the outcome.
7. Escalate any unresolved issue with the relevant technical information.

## What I Learned

This experience strengthened my ability to work independently in live customer environments. It improved my confidence with hardware replacement, Windows workstations, troubleshooting, technical documentation and customer communication.

The role also demonstrated the importance of completing post-installation testing rather than assuming that a replacement or configuration change had been successful.
